## Brand & Style

This design system channels an editorial, quiet-luxury ethos tailored for artisanal interior spaces and boutique home décor. Rooted in tactile materiality, Mediterranean architectural niches, and warm sun-bleached ceramics, the interface behaves like an upscale art catalog rather than a transactional store. 

The aesthetic philosophy centers on:
- **Architectural Minimalism**: Expansive ivory canvas, intentional asymmetry, generous negative space, and curated compositions that let high-resolution artisanal photography breathe.
- **Understated Materiality**: Soft sand overlays, warm earth tones, and delicate hairline separators instead of artificial gradients or heavy card surfaces.
- **Restraint & Poise**: Subtle, precise micro-interactions with gentle transitions. Opulent cues come from high-contrast serif headlines, balanced proportions, and curated craft details rather than overt or generic ornamentation.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from carved stone, kiln-fired terracotta, warm sand, and artisanal glassware:
- **Primary (`#98704D`)**: Muted Amber. Used selectively for purposeful focal points, active state highlights, curated badges, and high-impact calls to action.
- **Secondary (`#292821`)**: Deep Charcoal. Grounded, warm-black used for crisp typography, high-contrast buttons, structured architectural lines, and deep ground surfaces.
- **Tertiary (`#D8C6AC`)**: Soft Sand. Serves as container fills, subtle hover layers, chip backgrounds, and divider accents that bridge foreground elements to background stone.
- **Neutral (`#F6F2EA`)**: Warm Ivory. The primary canvas substrate evoking unbleached linen and polished plaster.

### Functional Roles & Contrast
- **Surface Background**: `#F6F2EA` provides a tactile, light atmosphere that prevents optical glare.
- **Surface Layer 1**: `#EFE9DD` for subtly nested cards and product pedestals.
- **Hairline Borders**: `#E3DAC9` and `#D8C6AC` at 60% opacity for precise, delicate partitioning.
- **Text Primary**: `#292821` provides uncompromised contrast against the ivory background.
- **Text Muted**: `#7B7467` for metadata, dimensions, and secondary editorial descriptions.

## Typography

The typographic tension pairs the classical, high-contrast romance of Playfair Display with the clean, contemporary clarity of Plus Jakarta Sans.

- **Headlines & Editorial Titles**: Rendered in Playfair Display to establish an upscale gallery feel. Editorial headlines preserve balanced letterforms with natural proportions and comfortable line heights.
- **Labels, Navigation, and Pricing**: Set in Plus Jakarta Sans. Uppercase micro-copy (`label-caps`, `label-nav`) features generous letter-spacing (`0.10em` to `0.14em`), conferring an architectural, high-fashion cadence.
- **Body & Storytelling**: Balanced weights with open counters ensure maximum legibility for artisanal provenance notes, dimensions, and craft details.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adopts an editorial fluid grid, alternating between strict structural alignment and rhythmic asymmetric focal blocks.

- **Grid Architecture**: 12-column layout on desktop with `2.5rem` gutters, collapsing to 6 columns on tablet and 2 or 4 columns on mobile.
- **Canvas Margins**: Deep outer margins (`4.5rem` on desktop) anchor content inward, providing an exclusive showroom frame.
- **Asymmetric Offsets**: Select product hero galleries use 7/5 or 8/4 column splits where text and imagery intentionally counter-balance each other, echoing architectural niches and gallery displays.
- **Vertical Flow**: Significant inter-section vertical breathing room (`space-2xl` / `5rem` to `8rem`) ensures that each curated collection feels like an individual exhibition.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain quiet luxury and architectural authenticity, avoid heavy drop shadows, harsh plastic blurs, and glossy gradients.

- **Tonal Layering**: Depth is achieved predominantly through muted surface shifts. A container placed over Warm Ivory (`#F6F2EA`) adopts Soft Sand tinting (`#EFE9DD` or `#E6DFC7`) rather than an artificial shadow.
- **Low-Contrast Hairlines**: Thin, 1px structural borders in `#D8C6AC` or `#292821` (at 12–20% opacity) separate elements cleanly without visual weight.
- **Ambient Ceramic Occlusion**: When depth is required for floating navigation or quick-view drawers, utilize an ultra-diffused, ambient shadow tinted with warm umber:
  `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -12px rgba(41, 40, 33, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(41, 40, 33, 0.04);`

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined, tailored architectural profile through crisp corners softened by subtle, nearly imperceptible curvature:
- **Base Corner Radius (`0.25rem` / `4px`)**: Applied to interactive inputs, cards, image frames, and buttons. This avoids brutalist harshness while remaining disciplined and avoiding playful, overly rounded shapes.
- **Contained Modules**: Secondary utility badges and subtle detail chips scale up to a maximum radius of `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`).
- **Pill Shapes Prohibited**: Pill buttons, oversized floating bubbles, and heavily rounded surfaces are strictly excluded to preserve an architectural silhouette.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: `#292821` background, `#F6F2EA` text, 0.25rem border-radius. Uppercase `label-nav` typography with generous tracking. Padding: `0.875rem 2rem`. Subtle hover transition to `#98704D`.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Transparent background, 1px border in `#292821` (or `#D8C6AC` on dark surfaces), text in `#292821`. Hover fills gently with `#EFE9DD`.
- **Editorial Text Link**: Inline text link accompanied by a fine bottom border underline with an offset of 4px.

### Product Cards
- **Structure**: Borderless container over `#F6F2EA` with an aspect ratio of 3:4 or 4:5 for imagery. Background of the image frame sits in `#EFE9DD`.
- **Content Hierarchy**: 
  1. Micro-label craft category in uppercase (`label-caps`, color `#98704D`).
  2. Product title in `title-editorial` (`Playfair Display`, color `#292821`).
  3. Price set in `label-price` (`Plus Jakarta Sans`).
- **Interaction**: On hover, image scales imperceptibly (1.02x scale, 400ms ease) with an understated secondary image dissolve. No pop-out drop shadows.

### Chips & Badges
- **Editorial Badges**: Background `#D8C6AC` at 35% opacity, text `#292821`, 1px border `#D8C6AC`. Sizing: `0.25rem 0.75rem`, letter spacing `0.12em` uppercase.
- **Filter Chips**: Soft sand background with 1px border in `#E3DAC9`. Selected state adopts `#292821` background with `#F6F2EA` text.

### Input Fields
- **Text Fields**: Subdued warm background `#FAF8F5`, bottom-only or delicate 4-sided 1px border in `#D8C6AC`. 0.25rem corner radius. Padding: `0.75rem 1rem`. Focus state transitions border to `#98704D` with zero glow rings.
- **Labels**: Floating or top-aligned `label-caps` in `#7B7467`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes**: Crisp 16px square with 2px radius, 1px border `#7B7467`. Checked state fills `#292821` with a fine ivory checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons**: Crisp concentric circle. Selected state shows a solid `#292821` center dot separated by a 2px ivory buffer.

### Gallery Pedestals & Niches
- Dedicated editorial component for curated collections: Asymmetric block modules styled like gallery alcoves, utilizing alternating spans across the grid with inset photography and contextual artisan notes.
